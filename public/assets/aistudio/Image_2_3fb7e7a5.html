Academic Stress

Feeling overwhelmed by coursework, exams, or project deadlines. It's common to feel pressure to perform, which can impact sleep and focus.

psychology

Anxiety

Experiencing persistent worry, racing thoughts, or physical symptoms like a fast heartbeat. It can make everyday situations feel daunting.

cloud

Depression

A prolonged sense of sadness, loss of interest in activities you usually enjoy, or feeling a lack of energy and motivation.

groups

Social Anxiety

Intense fear of being judged or scrutinized in social situations, leading to avoidance of peer interactions or public speaking.

person

Loneliness

Feeling disconnected from others, even when surrounded by people. A sense of isolation or lacking meaningful, close relationships.

work

Career Anxiety

Uncertainty or severe stress regarding future job prospects, placements, or choosing the 'right' career path in engineering.
