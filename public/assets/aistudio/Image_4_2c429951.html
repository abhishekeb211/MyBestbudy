Academic Stress

Academic stress is that overwhelming feeling when coursework, exams, or deadlines start to pile up. It's completely normal to feel this pressure, but you don't have to carry it alone. Let's work through it together.

CBT Strategy: Thought Reframing

When stressed, our minds often jump to worst-case scenarios. Let's practice challenging those thoughts.

cancel

Negative Thought

"If I mess up this assignment, I'm going to fail the whole course."

check_circle

Reframed Thought

"This assignment is just one part of my grade. I will do my best, and I can seek help if I struggle."

balance

REBT Insight: Preferences vs. Demands

Using the ABC model, we can change rigid demands ("I MUST get an A") into flexible preferences to reduce anxiety.

The Demand

"I MUST get a perfect score, otherwise I am a failure."

The Preference

"I would PREFER to do well, but I can handle it if I don't. My worth isn't tied to one grade."
